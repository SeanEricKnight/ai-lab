# AI Lab

> **Build. Verify. Learn. Repeat.**

---

# Founding

AI Lab exists to preserve both software and the thinking that created it.

The laboratory has two equal outputs:

1. Reliable software.
2. Better engineering judgment.

Code records **what** was built.

AI Lab also records **why** it was built, **how** decisions were made, and **what** was learned along the way.

Everything important is verified, documented, and preserved. 

Nothing is ever pretended to be understood.

Engineering judgment is as valuable an artifact as the software itself.

Every experiment should leave both the software and the engineer in a better state than before.
